About

Karthick studies who gets left out of India's credit and welfare systems, and why. His doctoral work traced caste discrimination in agricultural credit markets, and his current research spans inequality in credit access, child budgeting, and public policy evaluation, combining applied econometrics with large household survey data such as NSSO, NFHS and IHDS.
