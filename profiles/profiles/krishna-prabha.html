About

Krishna Prabha teaches English at Kristu Jayanti and works at the crossing point of cultural studies and traditional ecological knowledge — bringing together literary and cultural analysis with the study of indigenous and community-held environmental wisdom. She founded MITTALIS Research India, which she leads as President.
