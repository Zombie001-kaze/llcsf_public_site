About

Prof. Kaliappa Kalirajan is an internationally distinguished applied economist, econometrician, and policy analyst. He serves as Emeritus Professor of International and Development Economics at the Crawford School of Public Policy, The Australian National University (ANU). An alumnus of Madurai University and ANU (PhD, 1979), he has held major academic and leadership roles including Deputy Executive Director of the Australia South Asia Research Centre (ASARC) at ANU and Professor of International Economics at FASID/GRIPS in Tokyo. He has also been deeply affiliated as an honorary scholar and academic collaborator with the Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru.

His seminal research has made landmark contributions to applied econometrics—particularly in the methodology and estimation of stochastic frontier production functions, technical efficiency measurement, and total factor productivity (TFP) growth. Over a career spanning more than four decades, his scholarship bridges theoretical econometric modelling with practical macroeconomic and development policy, addressing regional economic integration (including RCEP and ASEAN), trade reforms, poverty reduction, agricultural economics, green growth, and climate change mitigation frameworks across Asia.
