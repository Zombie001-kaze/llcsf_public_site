About

Tony works at the intersection of literature and gender, examining how masculinity is written, read and contested across South Asian texts and cultural discourse. As an early-career researcher and teacher at BITS Pilani, he moves between mentoring PhD scholars and designing electives that put comparative literature and queer-feminist theory in conversation with students encountering these ideas for the first time.
