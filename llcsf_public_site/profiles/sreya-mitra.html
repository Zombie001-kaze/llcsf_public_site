About

Sreya studies how Bollywood travels — across screens, platforms, and borders. Her work examines popular Indian cinema, television and new media in South Asia, tracing questions of stardom, celebrity culture, fandom, gender and globalization as they play out in the culture industries of India and its diasporas.
