Academic Concept Note

Navigating the Labyrinth of 21st Century Futures

This conference brings together scholars, researchers, academicians and students to explore the intersections of literature, language, culture and the social sciences in shaping sustainable futures. It examines how literature, media, technology and interdisciplinary knowledge can contribute to more inclusive, ethical and equitable societies amid technological transformation, environmental crises, migration, digital cultures, social inequalities and evolving pedagogies.

"Through interdisciplinary and international dialogue, the conference foregrounds the continuing relevance of the humanities and social sciences in understanding contemporary global challenges."

Through interdisciplinary and international dialogue, the conference foregrounds the continuing relevance of the humanities and social sciences in understanding contemporary global challenges. It seeks to foster critical perspectives, meaningful collaborations and new approaches to knowledge production that can contribute to more sustainable, inclusive and equitable futures.

Organized by the Department of Languages (English), St. Francis de Sales College (Autonomous), Bengaluru, in academic collaboration with the Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru.

🌱 Environmental Ethics

Ecocritical dialogues bridging literature and traditional environmental knowledge.

Critical frameworks examining representations of gender, sexuality, and power.

🎬 Screen & Media Cultures

Cinema, digital ecosystems, and audience subjectivities in South Asia.

📊 Socio-Economic Policy

Interdisciplinary evidence from ISEC informing inclusive sustainability.

Media & Cultural Studies

Dr. Sreya Mitra

Associate Professor
American University of Sharjah

Dr. Sreya Mitra is an Associate Professor of Media Studies and Communication Theory at the American University of Sharjah. She holds a PhD in Media & Cultural Studies from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Her research interests include stardom and celebrity studies, gender, popular culture, and contemporary Indian film and television. She is actively engaged with international scholarly associations in media and cultural studies.

Keynote / Research Focus: "Screen Cultures, Public Imaginations & Mediated Futures in South Asia"

Humanities & Social Sciences

Dr. Tony Sebastian

Assistant Professor, Dept. of Humanities and Social Sciences
BITS Pilani Hyderabad Campus

Dr. Tony Sebastian is an early-career researcher whose scholarship focuses on Masculinity Studies, the Progressive Writers’ Movement, and representations of gender and sexuality in South Asian literature and film. His research engages with questions of gender, cultural politics, literary studies, and contemporary South Asian cultural production.

Applied Economics & Policy

Dr. Karthick V

Assistant Professor of Economics, Centre for Economic Studies and Policy
Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru

Dr. Karthick V is an Assistant Professor of Economics at the Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru. He holds a PhD in Economics from ISEC, with research focusing on caste discrimination in agricultural credit markets in India. His research interests include applied economics, inequality, social exclusion, agricultural credit, public policy, development economics, and applied econometrics. His work also engages with sustainable development, climate change, and inclusive economic growth, with publications in Scopus-indexed and other peer-reviewed journals.

Keynote / Research Focus: "Fiscal Architecture for Sustainable Transitions: Evidence from Indian States"
Applied Economics Inequality & Social Exclusion Agricultural Credit Sustainable Development

Interdisciplinary Trajectories

Conference Tracks & Thematic Areas

Six comprehensive academic streams bridging the humanities, cultural studies, social policy, and media ecosystems.

[ TRACK 01 ]

Literature and Gender Studies

Trauma and memory studies, mental health narratives, feminist & masculinity studies, queer and asexuality discourses, reproductive justice, and body representation in literature.

[ TRACK 02 ]

Media, Technology, and Digital Cultures

AI cultures, algorithmic governance, platform capitalism, digital privacy, OTT and streaming narratives, gaming cultures, podcasting, computational literary studies, and AI translation.

[ TRACK 03 ]

Ecocriticism, Posthuman Studies and Environmental Humanities

Green and blue humanities, waste and toxicity studies, multispecies ethics, climate displacement narratives, eco-cinema, biopunk, solarpunk, cyberpunk, and posthuman bodies.

[ TRACK 04 ]

Diaspora, Marginality, and Cultural Memory

Diaspora and virtual homelands, migrant subjectivities, border narratives, caste and intersectionality, subaltern digital publics, and archive activism in cultural memory.

[ TRACK 05 ]

Culture Studies

K-pop and global youth fandoms, sustainable consumption, fashion and food studies, health humanities, surveillance cultures, biopolitics, graphic narratives, and comic studies.

[ TRACK 06 ]

Interdisciplinary Humanities, Pedagogy, and Knowledge Futures

Interdisciplinary methodology, ELT and curriculum design, Education for Sustainable Development (ESD), digital pedagogies, and contemporary directions in critical theory.
