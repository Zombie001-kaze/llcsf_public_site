Academic Concept Note

Navigating the Labyrinth of 21st Century Futures

The contemporary world stands at the intersection of unprecedented technological accelerations, ecological precarity, and profound renegotiations of culture and gender identity. In this landscape, the metaphor of the Labyrinth serves not as a symbol of entrapment, but as a space of intellectual inquiry, transformative paths, and creative emergence.

"Literature and the humanities provide the compass; cultural and social sciences chart the pathways toward just, resilient, and sustainable futures."

Organized by the Department of Languages (English), St. Francis de Sales College (Autonomous), Bengaluru, in confirmed academic collaboration with the Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru, LLCSF 2026 brings together distinguished scholars, early-career researchers, and practitioners across disciplines to question how narratives reshape human relationships with the earth, technology, and one another.

🌱 Environmental Ethics

Ecocritical dialogues bridging literature and traditional environmental knowledge.

⚧ Gender & Masculinities

Critical frameworks examining representations of gender, sexuality, and power.

🎬 Screen & Media Cultures

Cinema, digital ecosystems, and audience subjectivities in South Asia.

📊 Socio-Economic Policy

Interdisciplinary evidence from ISEC informing inclusive sustainability.

Interdisciplinary Trajectories

Conference Tracks & Thematic Areas

Six comprehensive academic streams bridging the humanities, cultural studies, social policy, and media ecosystems.

[ TRACK 01 ]

Literature and Gender Studies

Trauma and memory studies, mental health narratives, feminist & masculinity studies, queer and asexuality discourses, reproductive justice, and body representation in literature.

[ TRACK 02 ]

Media, Technology, and Digital Cultures

AI cultures, algorithmic governance, platform capitalism, digital privacy, OTT and streaming narratives, gaming cultures, podcasting, computational literary studies, and AI translation.

[ TRACK 03 ]

Ecocriticism, Posthuman Studies and Environmental Humanities

Green and blue humanities, waste and toxicity studies, multispecies ethics, climate displacement narratives, eco-cinema, biopunk, solarpunk, cyberpunk, and posthuman bodies.

[ TRACK 04 ]

Diaspora, Marginality, and Cultural Memory

Diaspora and virtual homelands, migrant subjectivities, border narratives, caste and intersectionality, subaltern digital publics, and archive activism in cultural memory.

[ TRACK 05 ]

Culture Studies

K-pop and global youth fandoms, sustainable consumption, fashion and food studies, health humanities, surveillance cultures, biopolitics, graphic narratives, and comic studies.

[ TRACK 06 ]

Interdisciplinary Humanities, Pedagogy, and Knowledge Futures

Interdisciplinary methodology, ELT and curriculum design, Education for Sustainable Development (ESD), digital pedagogies, and contemporary directions in critical theory.
